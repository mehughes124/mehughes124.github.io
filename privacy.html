Privacy policy

Your data stays yours.

None of my currently published extensions - Hum, Package Tracker, Webcam Mirror or Heft - transmit, store or otherwise use any user data to 3rd parties. We load Google Analytics to track usage on the Hum extension, but that data is not used for any commercial purposes. These extensions do not collect personal user information of any kind.

Heft

Heft stores your chosen text size, boldness level and keyboard-shortcut preference for each site, plus a single on/off flag for its optional console logging. All of it is kept on your own device using Chrome's local extension storage. It is never synced or uploaded, and I cannot see it. Heft has no analytics, makes no network requests, and only changes how text is styled on the pages you visit - it does not read, collect or transmit page content. To clear your settings for a site, open the Heft popup there and choose “Reset this site”; uninstalling Heft deletes all of its stored settings.
